About Premiere Electric

Premiere Electric is a professional electrical contracting firm serving the tri-county area with expertise in both residential and commercial electrical services.

We proudly serve over $100,000 worth of existing customers from our predecessor companies, Gardner and Miller. Our commitment to quality workmanship and customer satisfaction has earned us the trust of the community.

Founded with a vision to provide exceptional electrical services, we combine years of industry experience with modern techniques and safety standards. Our owner-operated model ensures personal attention to every project.

Residential Wiring

Commercial Electrical
