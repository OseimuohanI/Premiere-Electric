Our Story

Building on a Strong Foundation

Premiere Electric was established to serve the valued customers of Gardner and Miller, respected electrical contracting businesses in the tri-county area. We inherited not just customer relationships, but a legacy of quality and reliability built over decades.

Our Growing Presence

With over $100,000 in existing customer relationships, we've built our foundation on the trust placed in us. As our owner, Robin, embarked on this venture, she recognized the opportunity to expand services to new customers while maintaining the excellence that existing clients expect.

Expansion and Growth

Starting as an owner-operated business, Premiere Electric is positioned for sustainable growth. As demand for our services increases, we're strategically hiring experienced electricians to expand our capacity while maintaining our commitment to personalized, quality service. Our growth strategy focuses on deepening relationships with existing customers and responsibly expanding into new markets.

Service Area

We proudly serve the tri-county area with comprehensive electrical contracting services. Whether you're a long-time customer from our predecessor companies or discovering Premiere Electric for the first time, we're committed to exceeding your expectations with every project.

Leadership & Team
